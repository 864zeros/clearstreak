ClearStreak

A private, offline recovery companion that lives entirely on your phone — where your sobriety is yours alone, and no one else can ever see it.

“Your recovery is yours alone. We cannot see it, sell it, subpoena it, or lose it.”

Why it exists

Recovery is the last place you should be surveilled.

Most recovery apps run on the surveillance economy: accounts, cloud sync, analytics, and data that quietly becomes someone else's asset. That means the most sensitive record a person keeps — their cravings, their slips, their reasons for staying clean — sits on a server that can be breached, sold, subpoenaed, or simply mined.

ClearStreak is built on the opposite premise. It keeps a person's recovery on their own device, encrypted and offline, and gives them a companion that treats them with dignity instead of tracking them for profit. No accounts. No cloud. No telemetry. Nothing to leak, because nothing ever leaves the phone.

Tools that serve the person — not the platform.

864zeros builds local-first software: utilities that work for you, on your device, without an account or a subscription tollbooth between you and your own life. Recovery is where that principle matters most.

🕊️

Dignity over data

Privacy, by architecture

Not a privacy policy — a privacy design.

Most apps promise privacy in a document you have to trust. ClearStreak makes it structural: the app is built so that exposing your data isn't just against policy — it's technically impossible. Here's what that means in plain terms.

✈️

It has no way to reach the internet

The core build ships without the internet permission entirely. Nothing you write can be sent, synced, shared, or leaked online — the capability simply isn't there.

🔐

Your data is encrypted on the device

Everything sensitive is stored in an AES-256 encrypted database (SQLCipher). On the phone itself, it's unreadable scramble without the key.

👆

Only your body unlocks it

The encryption key is bound to your fingerprint or face and this phone's secure hardware (StrongBox / Trusted Execution Environment). There is no recovery password — which means nothing anyone could steal, guess, or compel.

☁️

Nothing goes to any cloud

No accounts, no backups, no server. Your recovery data is never copied off the device — not to us, not to Google, not anywhere. If it isn't on your phone, it doesn't exist.

📊

Zero telemetry, zero analytics

The app doesn't measure you. There are no trackers, no usage analytics, no ad SDKs — nothing watching what you do.

🙈

Invisible to onlookers

Screenshots and screen-recording are blocked, and the app hides its contents in the app switcher — so your recovery never flashes on screen by accident.

The recovery model

Built around how recovery actually works.

🧭

Multi-journey tracking

Track concurrent recoveries — substances, smoking, vaping, gambling, behavioral, or a custom goal — each with its own start date and running history.

🎚️

Four-tier check-ins HALT-aware

Clear, Passing, White-Knuckling, or Critical — a quick honest read of the moment, with optional HALT context (Hungry, Angry, Lonely, Tired). Critical routes straight to the rescue hub.

💚

“Data over shame” slip model

A slip resets the active streak but never deletes the past. Cumulative clean days, previous stretches, and your personal best are preserved — and a slip is immediately reframed toward the record you're already climbing back toward.

🏅

Milestones & a visual calendar

Permanent milestone badges and a high-contrast heatmap calendar make progress visible and earned — momentum you can see at a glance.

In the hardest moment

A rescue hub, one tap away.

When a craving turns critical, ClearStreak drops everything and opens a calm, full-screen Rescue hub — designed to be usable when someone is overwhelmed, and to always put a real human within one tap.

📞

Your people first

One-tap dialers for your sponsor and support person, always shown — with a gentle prompt to add them if you haven't yet.

🛟

National lifelines built in

SAMHSA's helpline, the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, Crisis Text Line, and specialized quit-lines for smoking and gambling.

🌬️

Ground the wave

A 60-second grounding timer and a 4-7-8 breathing circle with haptic pacing — eyes-free tools to let the craving crest and pass.

🕯️

Calm, not clinical

A deliberate long-press exit prevents accidental dismissal, and the whole screen is built to steady, not to alarm.

Riding out a craving

Distraction that treats the urge — without ever telling you what to do.

The craving toolkit is built on a strict rule: zero ingestion, zero advice. It never suggests consuming anything, and any words on screen are gentle awareness, never a command. Instead it uses the body and the senses to let an urge pass.

🫧

Pocket Anchor

A tactile, timed session with rhythmic vibration that chunks time into manageable pieces and resolves with a calming wave.

📦

Tactile box breather

A four-part breath guided by haptic ramps — inhale, hold, exhale, rest — so you can follow it without even looking.

🧩

Mind-occupying mini-games

Quick visuospatial games that crowd out craving imagery — and are automatically withheld from screen- or gaming-recovery journeys, so a tool never feeds the habit it's treating.

Reflect

Grounding and perspective, on your terms.

An optional, entirely offline library for the quieter side of recovery — always available, never pushed, and free of any doctrine you didn't ask for.

📖

Recovery reflections

An original library of short, modern recovery passages — written to carry the wisdom of early recovery literature in plain, contemporary language — browsable by theme, with a fresh passage each day.

🌅

Scripture & the Serenity Prayer

A daily verse and public-domain scripture for those who want it, with the Serenity Prayer close at hand — and an optional faith layer you can switch on or leave off.

Always in view

Your streak on the home screen.

A home-screen widget shows your current streak and next milestone at a glance — a quiet, daily reminder of how far you've come — reading only from the phone's own sandboxed storage.
