# ClearStreak — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 27, 2026
**App:** ClearStreak (Android)
**Publisher:** 864zeros LLC

ClearStreak is a **local-first** recovery companion. By default, your data stays on your
device. There is **no account, no server, no tracking, and no ads.** This policy describes
exactly what the app does and does not do with your information.

*This is a plain-language description of our actual practices. It is not legal or medical advice.*

---

## The short version

- Everything you create in ClearStreak — your journeys, check-ins, journal notes, streaks,
  and support contacts — is stored **only on your device**.
- Your journal notes and check-ins are held in an **encrypted database (AES-256 via SQLCipher)**
  that is unlocked with your device's biometric or screen lock.
- We do **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share your recovery data. There is no account to
  sign into and no backend to send it to.
- The app **cannot back your recovery data up to the cloud** — it is explicitly excluded from
  Android backup — so it never leaves the phone through us.

---

## What data ClearStreak stores (on your device only)

ClearStreak stores the information you enter so it can track your progress:

- **Journeys** — the habits/substances you're tracking, start dates, categories, and any custom label.
- **Check-ins** — urge level, HALT triggers, and optional **journal notes**.
- **Streak history and milestones** — derived from your check-ins.
- **Support contacts** — an optional sponsor / support person you choose to save for the crisis screen.
- **Settings** — your in-app preferences (e.g., which optional content is shown).

**Sensitive/health information.** Because recovery data is personal health information, your
check-ins and journal notes are stored in a **separately encrypted database** and are only
accessible after you unlock the app with your **fingerprint, face, or device PIN/pattern/password**.
This data stays on your device.

## What ClearStreak does NOT do

- **No accounts.** You never create a login. We hold no profile for you.
- **No analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels, cookies, fingerprinting, or advertising.**
- **No collection of your recovery data.** Your journeys, check-ins, and journal notes are
  never transmitted to us or any third party.
- **No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.**
- **No selling or sharing of any personal data.**

## Permissions the app uses, and why

- **Biometric / device credential** (`USE_BIOMETRIC`) — to lock and unlock your encrypted
  recovery data. Authentication is handled by Android; the app never sees your biometric.
- **Vibration** (`VIBRATE`) — for the haptic grounding tools (e.g., the breathing pacer).
- **Internet** (`INTERNET`) — present **only** in the Google Play version, and used **solely**
  for Google Play's one-time purchase check. **No recovery data is ever sent over the network.**
  (An optional air-gapped build has no internet permission at all.)

The crisis screen can hand off to your phone's own dialer, messaging, or maps app when you tap
a helpline or "find a meeting." ClearStreak does not place calls or send messages itself, and
it shares nothing with those apps beyond the phone number or search you tapped.

## Payments

The one-time unlock is processed by **Google Play Billing**. Google handles the transaction;
ClearStreak receives only a confirmation that a purchase was made. **We never see or store your
payment card details.** See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google processes payments.

## Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, **you are in control of it**:

- To delete your data, delete it in the app or **uninstall ClearStreak** — this removes the
  local databases from your device.
- We hold no server copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our end and no data-deletion
  request to file.

## Children

ClearStreak is intended for adults managing their own recovery and is **not directed at children**.
We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Not an emergency or medical service

ClearStreak provides supportive tools and public-domain content; it is **not a medical device
and not an emergency service.** The crisis screen surfaces third-party helplines (such as 988 and
SAMHSA) for convenience. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
